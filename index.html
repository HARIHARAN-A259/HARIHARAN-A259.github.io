01 — About

Background

I'm Hariharan, a student building at the intersection of artificial intelligence and applied software. My interest is in how large language models can be shaped — through prompting, retrieval, and system design — into tools that solve real, specific problems rather than generic demos.

"I'm less interested in what a model can do in the abstract, and more in what it can do for one real user, on one real problem."

My current work centers on the Claude API and the broader generative AI toolchain — experimenting with prompt design, retrieval-augmented systems, and the fundamentals of machine learning that sit underneath them. I document what I build and continue to expand this portfolio as new work is finished.
